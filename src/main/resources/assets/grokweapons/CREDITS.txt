The `grokweapons` namespace is where the "Minecraft Reimagined" resource pack by
Reijvi (https://www.patreon.com/c/Reijvi) puts its dagger / polearm / staff
models and textures. Run that pack on the server and its 3D models take over.

The files bundled here are only a plain-sprite fallback so the rpgstats weapons
still render as recognizable items when that pack isn't loaded. They are simple
placeholder art, not Reijvi's work.
